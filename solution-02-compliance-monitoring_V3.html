Case Study

How a $1.8B RIA cut compliance overhead by 45% in one quarter.

A Southeast-based RIA managing $1.8B across 320 client households was spending over 60 hours per month on manual compliance review and document preparation. Regulatory exposure was growing with each new hire.

The Situation

The compliance team manually reviewed thousands of transaction records, cross-referenced client suitability profiles, and assembled regulatory reports by hand each quarter. A single SEC examination request typically required two weeks of all-hands document retrieval.

The Intervention

We deployed Paragon Sentry across their portfolio management and CRM systems. AI continuously monitors for suitability drift, flags communications risks, and pre-builds examination-ready document packages on a rolling basis.

The Outcome — 90 Days In

Compliance review time dropped by 45%. The firm's next regulatory examination was completed in three days rather than two weeks. Two compliance staff were redeployed to higher-value client-facing roles.
