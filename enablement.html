Case Study

How a $2B RIA reclaimed 15 hours per advisor in 90 days.

A Midwest-based registered investment advisor with 14 advisors and $2.1B in AUM was losing prime client-facing hours to meeting prep, note-taking, and CRM admin. Here's what we changed.

The Situation

Advisors were spending 45–90 minutes preparing for each client meeting and another 30–60 minutes on follow-up admin. With 8–12 meetings per week per advisor, that meant nearly two full days lost to non-revenue work.

The Intervention

We deployed an integrated meeting intelligence workflow across Redtail, Microsoft 365, and a custom AI orchestration layer. Briefings auto-assemble two hours before each meeting; transcription and CRM updates run in real time.

The Outcome — 90 Days In

Advisors recovered 15 hours per week each. The firm scheduled 31% more client review meetings without adding headcount and onboarded 47 new households over the next two quarters.
