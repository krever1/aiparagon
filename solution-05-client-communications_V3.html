Case Study

How a $600M RIA eliminated 80% of routine admin and improved client satisfaction scores.

A wealth management firm in Dallas had 8 advisors each managing 140+ client relationships. Routine communications — check-ins, tax deadline reminders, birthday notes, life-event follow-ups — were falling through the cracks entirely.

The Situation

Advisors were manually drafting dozens of routine emails each week, often from memory. Life-event triggers were missed. Re-engagement of lapsed clients was nonexistent. The client experience was inconsistent and dependent entirely on individual advisor habits.

The Intervention

We deployed Paragon Touchpoint — a trigger-based communication engine integrated with their CRM — automatically detecting life events, portfolio milestones, tax deadlines, and re-engagement windows, then drafting and routing personalized messages in each advisor's voice for one-click approval.

The Outcome — 90 Days In

Routine communication admin dropped by 80%. Client satisfaction scores increased by 22 points. Three lapsed household relationships were reactivated within the first 60 days, representing $8.4M in retained AUM.
