    Call library

    Add transcripts of calls your team handled well. Answers, clarifying questions, escalation rules and team rules are drafted from them, and nothing reaches the agent until a person signs it off.

    The call library isn't switched on for the live data yet. Run vc-upgrade.sql in the Supabase SQL editor, then refresh.
    1Add a callUpload a transcript file, paste one, or pick a call the agent took.
    2DraftQuestions and answers, clarifying questions, escalations and rules are pulled out.
    3Sign offEdit anything, then approve or reject each one. Your name goes on it.
    4LiveAnswers apply from the next call. Rules and topic changes go out when you publish.
