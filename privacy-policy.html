AI Paragon Consulting Group ("AI Paragon," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information entrusted to us.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, access, use, process, disclose, retain, and protect information when individuals visit our website, communicate with us, or use applications, dashboards, integrations, and other services provided by AI Paragon.

This Privacy Policy also explains how AI Paragon handles information obtained through third-party integrations, including Google APIs.

1.Information We Collect and Process

Depending on how you interact with AI Paragon, we may collect or process the following categories of information:

Information you provide directly. This may include your name, email address, organization, job title, telephone number, information submitted through contact or consultation forms, and other information you voluntarily provide.

Account and authentication information. When you use an AI Paragon application or dashboard, we may process information necessary to authenticate you, maintain your authorized connection, provide application functionality, and protect the security of the application.

Google user data. When you choose to connect your Google account to an AI Paragon application, we may access information from Google services based on the permissions you authorize. Depending on the functionality enabled, this may include Google Calendar information and Gmail information.

Technical information. We may process limited technical information necessary to operate, secure, maintain, and troubleshoot our website and applications.

We seek to collect or access only information reasonably necessary to provide the functionality requested or authorized by the user.

2.Google Account and API Access

Certain AI Paragon applications allow users to connect their Google accounts to enable calendar, email, and related productivity functionality within an AI Paragon dashboard.

AI Paragon may use the Google Calendar API and Gmail API to provide these features.

Users connect their Google accounts through Google's authorization process and determine whether to grant the permissions requested by the application. AI Paragon does not receive or have access to a user's Google account password through this authorization process.

The specific Google information accessible to the application depends on the permissions and OAuth scopes authorized by the user.

3.Google Calendar Integration

When a user connects Google Calendar to an AI Paragon application, the application may access Google Calendar information necessary to provide calendar synchronization and calendar-management functionality.

Depending on the permissions granted by the user, this may allow the application to:

  • Retrieve and display relevant Google Calendar information within the dashboard;
  • Synchronize calendar events between Google Calendar and the dashboard;
  • Create or update calendar events when initiated or authorized by the user; and
  • Reflect relevant changes made in Google Calendar within the connected dashboard.

Google Calendar data is accessed and processed only as necessary to provide the calendar functionality requested or authorized by the user.

4.Gmail Integration

When a user connects their Google account and authorizes Gmail access, the AI Paragon application may access Gmail information necessary to provide email functionality within the dashboard.

Depending on the permissions granted by the user, the application may:

  • Retrieve and synchronize new emails for display within the dashboard;
  • Retrieve and display email messages and conversation threads;
  • Allow authorized users to create and edit email drafts within the dashboard; and
  • Send emails through the user's connected Gmail account when initiated or authorized by the user.

Gmail data is accessed only to provide these user-requested features and related application functionality.

AI Paragon does not use Gmail data for advertising, behavioral advertising, marketing profiling, or purposes unrelated to the functionality authorized by the user.

5.How We Use Google User Data

AI Paragon uses Google user data only to provide or support user-facing functionality requested or authorized by the user. This may include:

  • Authenticating and maintaining an authorized connection to Google services;
  • Retrieving and displaying authorized calendar information;
  • Synchronizing calendar information;
  • Creating or updating authorized calendar events;
  • Retrieving and displaying authorized email information and conversation threads;
  • Synchronizing new emails with the dashboard;
  • Creating or editing email drafts;
  • Sending emails when initiated or authorized by the user;
  • Maintaining the functionality and security of the integration; and
  • Troubleshooting authorized application functionality where necessary.

AI Paragon does not sell Google user data.

AI Paragon does not use Google user data for advertising, retargeting, behavioral advertising, or marketing profiling.

AI Paragon's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6.Storage and Processing of Google User Data

AI Paragon's Google integrations are designed primarily to facilitate authorized communication, synchronization, and functionality between a user's Google account and the applicable AI Paragon application.

AI Paragon does not maintain a separate persistent repository of Google Calendar or Gmail content for this integration.

Google user data may be retrieved and processed temporarily as necessary to:

  • Display authorized information within the dashboard;
  • Synchronize calendar or email information;
  • Create or update calendar events;
  • Display email messages or conversation threads;
  • Prepare email drafts;
  • Send user-authorized emails; or
  • Perform other functionality expressly authorized by the user.

Authentication information, such as OAuth access or refresh tokens, may be processed or retained where technically necessary to maintain the user's authorized connection to Google services. Such credentials are used only to provide and maintain the functionality authorized by the user.

7.Automation and n8n

AI Paragon may use n8n as part of the automation infrastructure supporting Google Calendar synchronization, Gmail functionality, and other authorized application workflows.

Where Google user data passes through an n8n workflow, it is processed only as necessary to perform the functionality requested or authorized by the user.

AI Paragon does not use n8n as a separate repository for storing Google Calendar or Gmail content as part of the integration described in this Privacy Policy.

8.Sharing and Disclosure of Google User Data

AI Paragon does not sell, rent, or provide Google user data to third parties for advertising, behavioral targeting, data brokerage, or unrelated marketing purposes.

Information may be processed by service providers or technology infrastructure providers where reasonably necessary to operate, secure, maintain, or provide the application functionality authorized by the user.

Google user data may also be disclosed where required by applicable law, regulation, court order, subpoena, or other valid legal process.

9.Artificial Intelligence and Automated Processing

Certain AI Paragon applications and services may incorporate artificial intelligence or automated technologies.

For the Google Calendar and Gmail functionality described in this Privacy Policy, Google user data will only be provided to an AI model or other AI service where doing so is necessary to provide a feature requested or authorized by the user and is consistent with applicable Google requirements.

AI Paragon does not use Google user data to train general-purpose artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

10.Human Access to Google User Data

AI Paragon personnel do not access a user's Google user data except where:

  • The user has provided permission or requested assistance requiring such access;
  • Access is necessary for security or troubleshooting and is consistent with applicable requirements;
  • Access is necessary to investigate fraud, abuse, or security incidents;
  • Access is required by applicable law; or
  • Such access is otherwise permitted under applicable Google policies.

Access, where required, is limited to the extent reasonably necessary for the applicable purpose.

11.Website Analytics and Advertising Tracking

AI Paragon does not currently use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, LinkedIn Insight Tag, Google Ads tracking, or similar advertising tracking technologies on its website.

We may use technical mechanisms necessary to maintain website functionality and security.

If our tracking or analytics practices materially change, we may update this Privacy Policy accordingly.

12.Data Security

AI Paragon uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, destruction, or misuse.

These measures may include authentication controls, access restrictions, secure connections, credential protection, and other safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information processed.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure, and therefore we cannot guarantee absolute security.

13.Data Retention

AI Paragon does not maintain a separate persistent repository of Google Calendar or Gmail content for the integration described in this Privacy Policy.

Google user data processed temporarily to perform authorized synchronization or application functionality is retained only as necessary to complete the applicable operation, subject to technical, security, and legal requirements.

Authentication information necessary to maintain an authorized connection may be retained for the duration of that authorized connection or until access is revoked, expires, or is otherwise terminated.

Other personal information provided directly to AI Paragon may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain legitimate business records, comply with applicable legal requirements, resolve disputes, or enforce agreements.

14.User Control, Revocation, and Deletion

Users control whether they connect their Google accounts to AI Paragon applications.

Users may revoke an AI Paragon application's access to their Google Account through their Google Account permissions settings.

Revoking access will prevent the applicable AI Paragon application from continuing to access Google data through the revoked authorization. Features that depend on the Google connection may no longer function after access is revoked.

Users may also contact AI Paragon to request deletion of applicable personal information maintained by AI Paragon, subject to information we are legally or operationally required or permitted to retain.

Privacy and deletion requests may be submitted to:

privacy@aiparagonconsulting.com

15.Third-Party Services

AI Paragon applications may integrate with third-party services, including Google and n8n.

Third-party services maintain their own privacy policies, terms, and security practices. Users should review applicable third-party policies when connecting or using those services.

This Privacy Policy describes AI Paragon's handling of information and does not replace the independent privacy policies or terms of third-party service providers.

16.Children's Privacy

AI Paragon's business applications and services are not directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through these services. If we become aware that such information has been collected contrary to applicable requirements, we will take appropriate steps to address it.

17.Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes to our applications, integrations, business practices, technology, or applicable legal requirements.

When we update this Privacy Policy, we will revise the "Last Updated" date displayed at the top of the policy.

Where required by applicable law, we may provide additional notice or obtain consent before materially changing how previously collected personal information is used.
