What We Build & Integrate: Paragon Catalyst

Three integrated workflows. One outcome: advisors in front of clients, not screens.

01

Automated Meeting Preparation

Assembles a complete client briefing before every call — CRM history, account snapshot, recent transactions, life events, and positions worth discussing. Advisors walk in fully prepared in under three minutes.

02

Real-Time Transcription & Commitment Capture

Joins every meeting, transcribes it, isolates the commitments on both sides, and routes action items with context to the right person before the call ends.

03

CRM Updates & Compliant Follow-Up

Notes, preferences, and next steps flow into the CRM automatically; personalized follow-up emails are drafted in the advisor's voice within firm tone and compliance standards.
