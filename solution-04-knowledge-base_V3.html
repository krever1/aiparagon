Case Study

How a 12-advisor RIA cut new hire onboarding from 8 weeks to 3.

A boutique wealth management firm in Chicago had deep institutional knowledge locked inside the heads of two founding partners. Every new hire required six to eight weeks of shadowing before operating independently.

The Situation

The firm had no documented SOPs, no searchable procedure library, and no way for junior advisors to find answers without interrupting senior staff. Tribal knowledge was the only knowledge management system, creating bottlenecks and single-person dependencies.

The Intervention

We deployed Paragon Atlas — building a secure, AI-searchable knowledge base from their existing documents, email threads, and recorded training sessions, then structuring it into queryable SOPs, compliance guides, and client communication templates.

The Outcome — 90 Days In

New hire onboarding dropped from eight weeks to three. Senior advisor interruptions for operational questions fell by 60%. The founding partners reclaimed an estimated 12 hours per week previously spent answering internal questions.
