BUSINESS IMPACT

What changes when everyone walks in prepared

Preparation stops depending on who owns the account and how much time they had that morning.

▣
Preparation stops being a person

The same five systems are read before every meeting, whether it is a first conversation or a customer of six years. Nobody has to remember, and cover is no longer a downgrade.

⇄
Commitments stop slipping

What was promised is recorded when it is made, checked on its own, and carried into the next brief if it is still open. Nothing depends on someone rereading the notes.

✓
Nothing rests on recollection

Every line points at a record, a thread, a note or a public announcement. What is missing is shown as missing rather than filled in with something plausible.
