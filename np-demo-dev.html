What's your role?

Spectrum shows a different workspace depending on your role. This sets what you see when you sign in — watch the panel on the right build as you answer.

What should we call your workspace?

This names the portal your staff and families will sign in to.

How many people do you support?

Everyone currently on your census, across all sites.

What would you like to get organized first?

Pick up to three. We'll bring these to the front of your workspace — everything else is still there when you want it.

How do you track all this today?

Honest answers get you a better starting setup. Most agencies pick the last one.

What's your role on the floor?

This sets what you can record — a shift lead files incident reports, a DSP writes the service notes behind them.

Who are you here for?

You'll only ever see the person you're authorized for — nobody else on the roster.

Enter your invite code

Your program sends this when they add you. It links you to the right workspace and nothing beyond it.
